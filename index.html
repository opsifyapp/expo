How shops use it

Nothing slips. Requests, estimates, parts and work orders live in one place, and parts carry through to the invoice.

Every hour counts. Employees log time, parts and photos on a phone app built for boats, so billing matches the work.

Customers in the loop. Customers see status, photos and job history. The shop reviews every update before it goes out.

Works with QuickBooks. Invoices keep going out the way they do now. Setup and migration are done with the shop.

Why now

Several shops in discovery said no software vendor had ever contacted them. Ten had dropped or resented a tool built for plumbers, auto shops or marinas. Many are booked 4 to 8 weeks out, growing, and spending 20 to 80 hours a week on admin.

The business

Monthly subscription per location, $100 to $300 a month by team size, QuickBooks included. Marine is the beachhead. Discovery found the same pain in landscaping, pool service and home repair, and a second vertical follows two marine case studies.

Team

John Gonsalves (founder and CEO) grew up around marine service and ran a marine detailing company in Annapolis for 4.5 years. Jesse Washburn (co-founder and CTO) brings years of production software engineering experience and has founded and exited a startup. TEDCO Pava LaPere Innovation Award recipient.

What's next
Growth beyond Maryland in 2027

The plan. A first hire, and taking the Annapolis in-person playbook to marine hubs beyond Maryland. Opsify is exploring angel investment and has not launched a round.

Looking for. Intros to marine shops outside Maryland, and people who have sold software to small service businesses.
